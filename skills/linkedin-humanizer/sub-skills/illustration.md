# Sub-skill: Image for a post

> **Suspended (11/10/2026).** Do not offer or generate images until the pipeline is
> adapted to the new design system. See `../../../references/strato-immagini.md`.

Images are no longer generated through Pixfaro. They are built with our own
pipeline: an HTML composition in the public brand look, rendered to PNG/JPEG,
approved together with the text and attached on publish.

Everything (rules, formats, where files live, how to attach):
`../../../references/strato-immagini.md` and
`../../../references/strato-browser.md` §Immagini da allegare.

## When this runs

- User says "add an image", "make a quote-card", "add a cover" for a post.
- Never offered proactively while the hold is on.
